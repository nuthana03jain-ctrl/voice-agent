How it works

Three simple steps, and a few minutes of conversation.

1

Talk

Tap the call button and allow your microphone. Archana greets you and starts the conversation.

2

Share your background

Tell her about your education, experience, skills and what you enjoy, in your own words.

3

Get paths and next steps

Hear career options that suit you, plus concrete next steps such as skills to build or roles to explore.

Ready to talk?

Look for the Archana call button in the bottom-right corner of this page. Tap it, allow microphone access, and start speaking.

Tip: use Chrome, Edge or Safari, find a quiet spot, and wear headphones for the best experience.
